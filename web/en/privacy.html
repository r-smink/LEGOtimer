Privacy policy

What BrickTimer does with your data, and what it doesn't. Last updated on 9 November 2026.

In short

  • Your builds, sessions and settings are stored only on your phone, unless you create an account.
  • With an account, your username, build times, builds and awards become public on the leaderboard.
  • The free version shows an ad when you start the timer, through Google AdMob. In the EU we ask for your consent first. With Premium (a one-time purchase) you won't see ads anymore.
  • No analytics or tracking of our own. We don't sell data.
  • You can delete your account and all shared data at any time.

Who with

Supabase

Supabase hosts the database and sign-in service, on servers in the EU. They process your data on our behalf and don't use it for their own purposes. Your password is stored encrypted; we can't see it.

OpenRouter

If you ask for a progress estimate after a session, the photos you pick are sent with your own OpenRouter key to the AI model you chose in the settings. BrickTimer doesn't keep those photos. What OpenRouter and the model provider do with them is described in OpenRouter's privacy policy.

Google AdMob

In the free version, Google AdMob shows an ad when you start the timer. Google uses your advertising ID and data about your device for this. In the EU you choose the first time whether you allow personalized ads; you can change that later under Settings → Ad privacy choices. How Google handles this is described in Google's policy. With Premium, AdMob is no longer loaded.

Rebrickable

The set catalog comes from Rebrickable. When you search, only your search term and filters are sent, nothing that can be traced back to you.

Your rights

Retention and deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account in the app (Ranking → Delete account), your account, profile and shared builds are gone immediately. They disappear from backups within 30 days. If you can't use the app anymore, request deletion here.

Access, correction and complaints

You can always ask which data we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. Email us at bricktimer@fyndable.ai. If you're not happy with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or the data protection authority in your country.

Children

If you are under 16, ask your parent or guardian for permission before creating an account.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Everyone can only change their own profile and builds.
