Features

Everything for your build sessions

⏱

One-tap timer

Start and stop your build session in the app or straight from your home screen with the widget. Forgot to time it? Add a session afterwards.

▦

Every LEGO set

Search the complete catalog by name, set number, theme or piece count. With image, pieces and minifigures.

▤

Your own portfolio

Put sets on your wishlist, pick your current build and see your total build time, sessions and seconds per brick for each set.

✦

AI progress

Take a photo of your build and an AI model estimates how far along you are, compared with the finished set.

★

Stars and titles

Eight challenges and three build habits, from Night Owl to SpeedBrick. Grow from Apprentice to Brickmaster.

◐

Your way

A calm dark theme for evening sessions, or light if you prefer. In English or Dutch, or simply following your phone.

Build against the rest

Create a free account and you're on the leaderboard. Compare build time, finished sets, bricks and the fastest time per brick, and check out other builders' builds and awards.
