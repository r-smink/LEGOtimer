Delete account

Your BrickTimer account and everything you shared, permanently gone.

Two ways

1In the app (instant)

Open BrickTimer and go to Ranking → Delete account. Confirm, and your account is deleted right away.

2By email

Don't have the app anymore? Send an email with your username from your account's email address. We delete your account within 30 days and let you know when it's done.

Request deletion
What happens

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, username and password
  • Your public profile: build time, sets, bricks, stars, titles and awards
  • All your shared builds on the leaderboard

What stays

  • Your builds and sessions on your own phone. They are stored locally, and you can keep using the app without an account.
  • Copies in backups, for at most 30 days. After that, those are gone too.

We don't keep anything longer, not even anonymously.
